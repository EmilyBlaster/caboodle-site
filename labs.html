the bench.

Three experiments in the spotlight, five more on the shelf below. Each has a live URL, a thesis, and a next step. Click through to the working version.

    LAB
    001
    parallax + scrollytelling · interaction design live · experiment

    reading the rainforest.

    Three layers of rainforest, canopy to floor, stacked into one continuous scroll. You start in the bright crown and sink to the dark floor, and the whole scene runs on a single input: how far down the page you are. No timeline, no play button. Scroll it live, in place, or open it full screen.

    Thesis: Most digital learning treats scroll as a way to get from one box to the next. But scroll is motion, and motion carries feeling. Testing what that means for how people move through, and remember, an experience.

    LAB
    002
    ai-built tool · workflow design live · 24-hour build

    inline stakeholder review system.

    A feedback workspace built directly into a client landing page using Claude Code. Pins anchored to exact positions, color-coded reviewers, a slide-out panel that groups comments by section with resolve tracking. No email threads. No annotated screenshots. No "jump on a quick call?"

    Thesis: Exploring whether AI-assisted code generation can solve real workflow problems mid-project, not just build features, but build tools, with a 24-hour turnaround and no design system to start from.

    LAB
    007
    applied llm + rag · self-directed live · solo build

    greenroom: a development copilot.

    An employee asks a real question in plain words. Greenroom answers from a curated people development library using real retrieval with citations, or an honest "not covered". It proposes a verb-first next step, then follows up to record whether the behavior actually changed. Built solo with Claude Code across six surfaces.

    Thesis: Most AI tools stop at delivering content. Greenroom is designed around the part they skip, behavior change, and proves it with a real eval harness: 22 of 22 cases pass, 100% refusal accuracy, and an independent judge model scoring groundedness at a mean of 92/100.
