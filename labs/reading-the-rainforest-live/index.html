Layer One · The Bright Crown

The Canopy

where the light lands first

Up here the forest is loud and bright. Spider monkeys swing hand over hand, macaws flash red and blue, and nearly every meal the jungle offers starts in these branches. The canopy catches the sun, the rain, and the fruit before anything else gets a turn.

Layer Two · The Middle Dark

The Understory

where patience hunts

Below the bright crown the light goes soft and broken. Here, things wait. A jaguar folds along a branch, a boa knots into the shade, and a frog holds dead still.

Layer Three · The Dark Floor

The Floor

where everything returns

All the way down, the light is almost gone. One shaft finds the floor. A beetle plows the litter, an agouti buries the seeds it forgets, and mushrooms turn the dead back into soil. Down here, nothing is wasted.
