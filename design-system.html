01
creative north star

the field journal.

This is the field journal of a learning designer who studies how humans actually change before she designs anything. The page is warm paper, not a screen: an off-white ground carrying a faint grain, the way a notebook page carries tooth. On top of it, two voices work in tension. Bauhaus Bool sets the display, lowercase and enormous, the confident hand lettering of someone who has an opinion. JetBrains Mono sets the chrome: field-note stamps, call numbers, specimen labels, timestamps. The mono is the anthropologist's marginalia, the evidence that the work was observed and catalogued, not asserted.

Work is filed, not displayed. Engagements carry call numbers. Lab experiments are specimens you power on in place. Color is used with discipline: paper and graphite carry the page, and the three brand colors arrive as signal on small percentages of any surface.

It rejects its neighbors on purpose. Not generic L&D. Not the AI-hype voice. Not the SaaS-cream portfolio. Not the hero-metric template. The proof is the work and the client list, framed as field evidence.

02
color

three signals. one paper.

A warm-neutral paper system carrying three high-signal brand colors, used sparingly, on a graphite-and-lime backbone. Ratios below are WCAG contrast against the page ground and against graphite ink. AA means 4.5:1 or better for text at any size, AA large means 3:1 or better and is only allowed at 24px and up, or for non-text elements.

ratios computed from the sRGB relative luminance formula in WCAG 2.2. paper ground #f2efe8 · ink #282828.

  • the paper ruleThe page ground is warm ash paper, never #fff and never pure #000. Every neutral is tinted warm. Pure white appears only as a focused input, a surface deliberately brighter than the page.
  • the small-magenta ruleBright editorial magenta clears only 3.7:1 on paper, so it is display and true accents only, at 24px and up. Any magenta text below that uses magenta ink (5.5:1 on paper) or magenta lift (5.7:1 on night ground).
  • the lime ruleSignal lime clears 9.6:1 on ink and only 1.3:1 on paper. It carries text only on dark grounds. On paper it is a dot, a bar, a fill behind graphite text, never a word.
  • the signal ruleLime, blue, and magenta are signal, not surface. On any given screen they occupy a small percentage. Rarity is what makes the lime land.

04
elevation

flat paper. long, soft lift.

Surfaces are flat at rest and lift in response to hover or focus, never decoratively. Shadows are large in blur, negative in spread, low in alpha, so a lifted card reads as a physical object on paper catching diffuse light. Depth is also carried tonally: paper shadow recesses a panel without any shadow at all.

  • the long-throw ruleIf a shadow looks tight, dark, or small-blur, it is wrong. No 2014-app drop shadows anywhere.

06
motion

one ease. three durations.

Everything moves on the same curve, an ease-out-expo family, so the site feels like one hand made it. Nothing loops, nothing bounces, numbers sit still. When a visitor asks for reduced motion, reveals resolve instantly and view transitions are disabled.

house easecubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)Default easing for every state change and reveal.
micro0.2sHovers, taps, the underline wipe.
ui0.4sNav, panels, button color flips.
reveal0.6sEntrances and scroll reveals. The lime curtain bar lifting off the hero headline.
07
accessibility

wcag 2.2 aa, checked, not claimed.

The site targets WCAG 2.2 AA. The specific commitments, so you can hold it to them:

  • text contrastGraphite on paper is 12.8:1. Ash gray was deepened from #686868 (4.9:1) to #5c5c5c (5.8:1) so secondary text clears 4.5:1 everywhere, including tinted panels.
  • brand color disciplineLime never carries text on paper. Bright magenta never sets text below 24px. Both rules exist because the ratios above say they have to.
  • focusEvery interactive element shows a 2px magenta focus ring at 2px offset on keyboard focus. On dark grounds the ring is magenta lift or lime.
  • skip linkThe first focusable element on every page is a skip link to the main content, visible on focus.
  • motionprefers-reduced-motion is honored in CSS and in script: reveals resolve at once, view transitions are turned off, autoplaying media is not relied on.
  • images and mediaMeaningful images carry alt text, decorative ones are hidden from assistive tech, below-the-fold images lazy-load with explicit dimensions so nothing shifts. Video demos ship with captions.
  • targets and structureMobile tap targets are 44px or larger. One h1 per page, and every skip link lands on the first content section.
08
do and don't

the short list.

do

  • Ground every page in warm ash paper with the grain layer. Tint every neutral warm.
  • Keep JetBrains Mono to small chrome: kickers, stamps, call numbers, captions.
  • Set the display lowercase and oversized, and let a lime curtain bar reveal it.
  • File work under call numbers with mono metadata, the field-journal way.
  • Use magenta ink or magenta lift for small magenta text. Reserve bright magenta for display.
  • Lift surfaces with long, soft, negative-spread shadows, only on hover or focus.

don't

  • Write "passionate about learning" energy, or any generic L&D filler.
  • Use the AI-hype voice: bare-category headlines, reinvention frames, trademarked-framework theater.
  • Ship a SaaS-cream portfolio, or the hero-metric template of a big number plus supporting stats.
  • Build identical card grids of icon plus heading plus text.
  • Use gradient text or glassmorphism. Emphasis comes from weight, size, and the brand colors.
  • Use an em dash anywhere except the official tagline.
  • Use pure #fff as a page ground or pure #000 for text.
