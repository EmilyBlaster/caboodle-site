# PRODUCT.md — caboodle design portfolio site

## Product Purpose
Premium portfolio and positioning site for caboodle design. Primary job: convert two distinct audiences from one site. (a) Fortune 500 transformation leads, AI program owners, and CLOs hiring fractional AI program leadership engagements. (b) Hiring managers looking for a full-time AI Program Lead with deep behavior change and learning systems credibility. Secondary: establish Emily Green as the credible, slightly-edgy alternative to both AI-hype consultancies and traditional L&D shops.

## Register
brand — this IS the product. Design is positioning.

## Users
- **Primary A — consulting buyer**: Transformation leads, CLOs, AI program owners, and program managers at Fortune 500 companies hiring fractional support for AI rollouts where behavior change is the actual bottleneck.
- **Primary B — full-time hiring manager**: Heads of People, transformation officers, and AI practice leads at companies hiring a full-time AI Program Lead.
- **Secondary**: Peers and collaborators (other designers, instructional designers, AI program practitioners) who may refer work or validate credibility.
- **Not**: Students, job seekers outside AI program leadership, anyone looking for "passionate about learning" energy.

## Strategic Principles
1. **AI programs that humans actually adopt** — every design and copy choice signals rigor about human adoption, not hype about the model.
2. **Behavior change over checkbox training** — preserved POV from the L&D era; still load-bearing for the new positioning.
3. **The translation layer** — Emily sits between AI capability and human adoption, between learning science and executive language. The site has to feel legible in all four worlds.
4. **Credibility-forward** — client names (Apple, Airbnb, GitLab, J&J, T-Mobile, Intuit) are proof points, not name drops.
5. **One scoped hero, dual signal in lower-priority surface** — the hero owns one frame (AI Program Lead). The availability surface on the about page names both modes (fractional + full-time) and gives each its own contact path.
6. **Direct, confident, slightly edgy** — no corporate jargon, no filler, no "passionate about" energy.

## Brand
- **Colors**: Lime Green #96E650 (primary), Deep Blue #03599B (secondary), Magenta #EC008C (tertiary), Dark text #282828, Mid text #686868, Paper/bg #f2efe8
- **Type**: Bauhaus Bool 600 for display/headings, DM Sans 400–600 for body/UI, JetBrains Mono for kickers/labels
- **Voice**: Direct, confident, slightly edgy, credibility-forward. Short sentences. Active voice. No em dashes (except the official tagline).
- **Tagline**: "Strategy, media, and behavioral science — it's the whole caboodle"

## Anti-references
- "Passionate about learning" energy — generic L&D positioning
- AI hype voice — bare-category H1s ("Generative AI"), aspirational reinvention frames, trademarked-framework theater. Big-firm AI practice voice does not translate to a solo whose credibility is the client list itself.
- SaaS cream portfolios — Notion-style white + one accent
- The hero-metric template — big number + supporting stats
- Identical card grids with icon + heading + text
- Gradient text or glassmorphism
- Corporate jargon or buzzword-heavy copy
- Diluted dual-audience CTAs that flatten both consulting and full-time signals
