# PRODUCT.md — caboodle design portfolio site

## Product Purpose
Premium portfolio and positioning site for caboodle design, a learning design consultancy. Primary job: convert Fortune 500 L&D buyers into booked discovery calls. Secondary: establish Emily Green as the credible, slightly-edgy alternative to checkbox-training consultancies.

## Register
brand — this IS the product. Design is positioning.

## Users
- **Primary**: L&D directors, CLOs, and program managers at Fortune 500 companies looking to hire a consultancy for high-stakes behavior-change programs.
- **Secondary**: Peers and collaborators (other designers, instructional designers) who may refer work or validate credibility.
- **Not**: Students, job seekers, or anyone looking for "passionate about learning" energy.

## Strategic Principles
1. **Behavior change over checkbox training** — every design and copy choice should signal rigor, not enthusiasm.
2. **The translation layer** — Emily sits between learning science and executive language. The site should feel like it belongs in both worlds.
3. **Credibility-forward** — client names (Apple, Airbnb, GitLab, J&J, T-Mobile, Intuit) are proof points, not name drops.
4. **Direct, confident, slightly edgy** — no corporate jargon, no filler, no "passionate about" energy.

## Brand
- **Colors**: Lime Green #96E650 (primary), Deep Blue #03599B (secondary), Magenta #EC008C (tertiary), Dark text #282828, Mid text #686868, Paper/bg #f2efe8
- **Type**: Bauhaus Bool 600 for display/headings, DM Sans 400–600 for body/UI, JetBrains Mono for kickers/labels
- **Voice**: Direct, confident, slightly edgy, credibility-forward. Short sentences. Active voice. No em dashes (except the official tagline).
- **Tagline**: "Strategy, media, and behavioral science — it's the whole caboodle"

## Anti-references
- "Passionate about learning" energy — generic L&D positioning
- SaaS cream portfolios — Notion-style white + one accent
- The hero-metric template — big number + supporting stats
- Identical card grids with icon + heading + text
- Gradient text or glassmorphism
- Corporate jargon or buzzword-heavy copy
